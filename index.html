Company Profile

Setting the Benchmark for Architectural Flooring Worldwide

Anupama Flooring is a flagship manufacturer and export house established with a single vision: marrying timeless earth timber aesthetics with state-of-the-art moisture-resistant polymer engineering.

Founded with artisanal woodworking roots, Anupama Flooring has evolved into an international supplier of certified engineered hardwoods, commercial SPC rigid-core tiles, and large-format vitrified surfaces. We serve international distributors, developers of 5-star hospitality resorts, and luxury residential projects across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia Pacific.

FSC® Certified Sustainable Timber
Zero Formaldehyde & Zero VOC
Bespoke Stain & Dimension Matching
Dedicated Maritime Export Packing

Our manufacturing facilities in Gujarat and Telangana feature advanced German HOMAG profiling lines, Italian automated UV coating systems, and high-pressure hydraulic presses. Each batch undergoes rigorous micro-caliper thickness testing, abrasion wear-layer testing (AC4/AC5 rated), and 72-hour moisture submersion verification.

HOMAG Micro-Bevel Extrusion
Italian Cefla 7-Coat UV Finish
Climate-Controlled Storage
In-House Physical & Chemical QA Lab

Environmental stewardship is at our core. 100% of our raw timber is harvested from responsibly managed, fast-growing eucalyptus and rubberwood plantations. Our SPC vinyl uses 100% recyclable virgin stone composite with zero heavy metals or phthalates, helping projects earn LEED & Green Building credits.
