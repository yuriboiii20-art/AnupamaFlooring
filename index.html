Company Profile

Born from Earth, Refined for Modern Living

At Anupama Flooring, we believe authentic flooring should never feel synthetic or mass-replicated. We harvest with reverence, dry slowly under the sun, and enhance timber with cold-pressed natural linseed oils that nourish the organic fibers rather than seal them beneath plastic films.

From multi-layered engineered planks with cross-laminated eucalyptus cores to natural stone terrazzo composites, our craft combines time-tested Indian woodworking traditions with German precision click-milling.

Hand-Scraped Textures

Revealing authentic wood grain depth.

Cold-Pressed Plant Oils

Satin matte finish with zero chemical odors.

Every log is traced from registered plantations in Gujarat and Southern India. Logs undergo a 60-day natural moisture equalization cycle followed by computer-monitored solar kiln drying to eliminate tension before milling.

Solar Kiln Seasoned

Guaranteed 8-10% equilibrium moisture.

Cross-Grain Stability

Eliminates seasonal expansion & buckling.

Zero waste leaves our workshops. Wood trimmings and sawdust are compressed into clean biomass briquettes that power our drying kilns, closing the loop on our carbon footprint.

Biomass Energy Cycle

100% sawdust repurposed for kiln heat.

Biodegradable Packing

Recyclable ocean container palletizing.

Why Choose Us

Grounded in Ecological Integrity

We reject quick artificial fixes in favor of generational timber durability, honest environmental provenance, and transparent export practices.

Responsible Forestry

Harvested strictly from managed timber farms. 3 saplings replanted for every mature tree to nurture forest biodiversity.

Plant-Based Finishes

Natural cold-pressed linseed and plant waxes allow wood to breathe without releasing harmful indoor formaldehyde.

German Precision Milling

HOMAG micro-bevel profiling creates tight moisture-locked joints that resist seasonal gapping and uneven step wear.

Natural Thermal Acoustics

Organic cellular timber and natural cork underlays absorb impact sound and maintain gentle foot temperature through winter.

Direct Port Logistics

Desiccant-protected and fumigated export palletizing with FOB / CIF delivery terms to major ports across Europe, the US, and Gulf.

Generational Longevity

Thick wear layers that can be sanded and re-oiled multiple times over decades, aging with a rich, unique natural patina.
