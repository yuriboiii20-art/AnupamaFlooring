Visionary Entrepreneur • Managing Director

Bhavna Aggarwal

B.Com • Diploma in Interior Design (Jenson & Nicholson India Ltd)

Bhavna Aggarwal comes from an established business family with diverse enterprise portfolios spanning Residential and Commercial Construction, Granite Quarry operations, specialized showrooms, and high-end Interior Fitouts across domestic and international markets.

She holds a Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) degree alongside a professional diploma in Interior Design from Jenson & Nicholson India Ltd. A dynamic and accomplished businesswoman with a formidable entrepreneurial spirit, she has successfully founded and led a versatile portfolio of multi-industry companies over the past 25 years.

Her strategic acumen and visionary leadership continue to guide large-scale infrastructure projects, land development initiatives, vanlines logistics, and global organic export ventures.

• Residential & Commercial Construction • Granite Quarry & Showrooms • Interior Fitouts & Design • Land Development • Domestic & Global Trade
