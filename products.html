Our Process: Farm to Container

From ethical cattle sourcing on partner farms to ocean container loading, every step is rigorously controlled.

1

Collection

from healthy cattle on partner farms

2

Cleaning

to remove stones, straw and debris

3

Shaping

into uniform cakes, or crushing into powder

4

Sun-drying

for several days, turned regularly, so moisture drops to a safe level

5

Quality checks

for dryness, size and cleanliness

6

Packing

in moisture-resistant bags or cartons

Good drying is the most important step. Moisture left in the product leads to mould, extra weight and spoilage on long journeys, so we dry thoroughly and check every batch.
