FARMDUNGEXPORTER • A Unity of ABHIELITE GROUP PVT LTD

India’s Trusted Exporter of Organic Cow Dung Products

We specialize in producing and exporting high-quality, 100% organic cow dung products sourced directly from rural farms in India under FARMDUNGEXPORTER, a unity of ABHIELITE GROUP PVT LTD. Our products are naturally processed, chemical-free, and widely used for agriculture, religious rituals, and eco-friendly fuel across global markets.

With a strong commitment to quality and sustainability, we follow traditional sun-drying and hygienic processing methods to preserve the natural properties of our products. Each batch is carefully inspected to maintain purity, consistency, and strict export-grade standards while meeting global compliance requirements.
